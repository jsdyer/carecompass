Know what the source can tell you

Age filters show providers explicitly marked for that group. Missing fields stay unknown. Under 3 combines the source’s infant/toddler or under-36-month categories; confirm the exact age range. Licence capacity is not the number of open spaces.

Search and filtering happen on this device. A shortlist is saved only when you choose “Add to shortlist”; contact notes stay in this browser’s local storage. It does not sync across devices. Map tiles and links you open contact their providers. Do not add children’s names or sensitive details.
